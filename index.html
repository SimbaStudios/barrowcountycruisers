About The Cruisers

Cars and community in Winder

Barrow County Cruisers hosts automotive events in Winder, Georgia featuring cars, trucks, bikes and other enthusiast vehicles. Events throughout the year have included themed shows and community-focused activities such as food and toy collections, family activities and vehicle awards. Whether you're bringing something to show or just stopping by to look around, Barrow County Cruisers events are about enjoying the vehicles and spending time with the community.
